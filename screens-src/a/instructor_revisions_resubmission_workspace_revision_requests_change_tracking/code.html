Institutional Charter Rule §4.2: Revision Scope Policy

Veritas Academia Faculty Governance Protocol

Section 4.2(a) — Permissible Scope of Revisions

Instructors are obligated to provide clarifying marginalia, reference guide citations, or explanation of rubric evaluation markers at no additional fee. Requests seeking clarification of ambiguous marginalia are always classified as Within Agreed Scope.

Section 4.2(b) — Exclusions & Redlines

A revision request may not demand direct prose ghostwriting, restructuring of unscoped supplementary appendices, or generation of artificial originality thresholds. Any such request must be rejected or escalated via Institutional Liaison Review.

Section 4.2(c) — Escrow Lock Parameters

Escrow remains locked in neutral ledger witness status during the revision inspection window. Upon resubmission, the student retains a 48-hour inspection grace period before automated settlement.
